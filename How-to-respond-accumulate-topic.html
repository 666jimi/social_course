How to accumulate conversation topics?

We've learned about mindset, goals, and eight core principles of conversation. If you consistently complete the after-class exercises, your conversational skills will definitely improve. However, this improvement may not be positive, because the previous lessons focused more on rules and regulations, telling you what not to do and what not to do. Some people can break free from these rules, and their conversations will immediately improve. But others will be restricted by these rules, feeling constrained and unsure how to talk. Starting with this lesson, we'll move into the methodological phase, showing you how to conversation effectively.

Core Starting Point: Share Your Life

Take dating as an example. We all know that in the early stages of a romantic conversation, it's usually just the man who initiates the topic and opens the conversation. Then, based on the woman's response, he expands on it, evokes emotions, generates more interaction, and ultimately creates a pleasant chat. However, many students are confused. They say, "Teacher, I also want to initiate topics, but I don't know what to talk about." At this point, I always give them a standard answer: share your life.

It sounds easy, right? But even something so easy, many people still can't do it. Because in their minds, two thoughts hinder their actions.

Misconception Case: Attracting with External Perks vs Your Own Personality

Student’s wrong idea: Take the girl to a John Mayer concert she likes to make her agree to the date.

Problem: You cannot judge if she agrees because of you or the concert. If she only comes for the concert, your personal charm cannot be verified.

Correct logic: Invite her for a casual walk. If she agrees, it proves she’s interested in you as a person, not external attractions. Stable attraction must rely on your unique personality, not high-end external topics or perks.

The second common wrong thought: "My daily routine is only work, meals and games, I have nothing worth sharing." This is the core pain point this lesson solves. Next we will learn three types of conversation topics and the three-tier progressive thinking method to create endless chat material.

The Three-Tier Progressive Thinking Method & Three Major Topic Categories

Not knowing what to talk about stems from lack of life observation and topic accumulation. Topics are everywhere in daily life, you only need conscious observation. We divide all available conversation topics into three core types:

1. Impromptu Conversation Topics (Things you see in front of you)

2. Stance-Based Conversation Topics (Values & personal life choices)

3. Timely Conspicuous Conversation Topics (Current trending hot events)

1. Impromptu Conversation Topics (80% of daily chat material)

Impromptu topics refer to all objects and events happening right in front of you. Any sight around you can be turned into rich chat content with the three-layer progressive thinking method.

Three progressive layers to expand any scene:

Layer 1: The thing / event itself (objective fact)

Layer 2: Physical sensory feelings brought by the thing

Layer 3: Inner psychological emotions & thoughts

Bad VS Good Impromptu Chat Example: Finished Dinner

Bad rigid interview style:
You: I just finished eating, how about you?
Her: Me too.
You: What did you eat?
Her: Noodles.
You: Was it tasty?
Dead end after short back-and-forth.

Good three-layer expansion version:
"I just had spaghetti tonight, it tasted really bland. I guess eating alone just doesn’t feel satisfying at all."

Covers event itself → physical taste feeling → inner lonely emotion, gives the other person lots of angles to respond and deepen the conversation naturally.

Extra Practice Examples of Three-Tier Thinking

Example 1: Ordered takeout
"I grabbed takeout for dinner today and it’s surprisingly delicious! I always thought takeout was greasy and bland, this shop’s cooking almost beats my own homemade meals."

Example 2: Uncomfortable office chair
"I’ve been sitting on this new chair all day, my waist is killing me. I’m gonna end up with a herniated disc at this rate."

Practice this thinking habit anytime you notice something in life, you will never run out of spontaneous topics.

2. Standing-Based Conversation Topics (Value & Life Stance)

Everyone has unique life stances and value orientations reflected in daily choices: consumption habits, hobbies, career preferences, life attitudes. Similar stances create closeness and resonance; different stances spark interesting discussion.

Core rule for stance topics: You don’t have to agree with their views, but you must fully respect them. Never directly negate or belittle their preferences when you are not close yet.

Taboo Example: Disrespecting Others' Preferences

She: I love watching soap operas, they’re really relaxing.
Wrong reply: "Soap operas are meaningless wastes of time, you should watch documentaries instead."

This kind of negation makes others feel judged and annoyed. Instead, share your own favorite shows to exchange perspectives peacefully without denying hers.

3. Conspicuous Timely Conversation Topics (Current Hot Trends)

These are era-exclusive trending content: new movies, celebrity news, viral variety shows, social media hot topics on Instagram/Twitter/Facebook. New trending material appears every day for unlimited chat supply.

You can also apply the three-tier progressive method to trending news to enrich the topic, and naturally reveal your personal stance during emotional expression, creating resonance with the other party.

Trending Hot Topic Expansion Example

"Did you see that celebrity cheating scandal that went viral? The photos leaked are shocking. I really can’t wrap my head around why someone with a perfect partner at home would mess around like that."

Includes event fact → sensory shock → inner opinion & stance, fully three-layer expanded.

Critical Taboo: Do Not Show Off Your Knowledge in Chat

Some people have extensive knowledge, but they ruin attraction by over-showing off professional jargon to look sophisticated. For example, rambling about wine terminology nonstop as soon as wine is mentioned, ignoring whether the girl understands or cares.

The core purpose of conversation topics is to create relaxed, pleasant interaction and advance your relationship, not to satisfy your desire to show off knowledge. Pretentious long lectures will create distance and reduce your charm significantly.

Full Lesson Summary

If you always feel you have nothing to talk about in chats, master this complete system:

1. Fundamental starting point: Share your real daily life instead of relying on external fancy topics.
2. Three core topic sources: Impromptu daily scenes, personal value stances, real-time trending hot topics.
3. Core expansion tool: Three-Tier Progressive Thinking Method to enrich any single small matter into a complete conversation.

Build the habit of observing life and practicing three-layer expansion thinking every day, and you will never run out of chat material with anyone. Always keep your core chat goal in mind, avoid meaningless knowledge showing off, and use topics to pull the distance between you and the other party closer.

Start daily training with this set of methods, and your problem of "having nothing to talk about" will be completely solved.
