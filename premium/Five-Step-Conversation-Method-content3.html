Course Introduction — Filtering & Screening Stage (Step 3 of Five-Step Chatting)

As mutual understanding deepens and the other person’s replies grow consistently positive, it proves you’ve generated basic attraction in the getting-to-know-each-other stage. When they grow interested in you, they will release clear interest signals divided into three progressive tiers from shallow to deep. Continuous appearance of these signals means you are ready to enter the screening stage to test whether their goodwill is genuine or merely polite social performance.

1. Shallow interest signal: Actively inquire about your personal experiences, inner thoughts and feelings (beyond basic surface information)

2. Medium interest signal: Take initiative to give targeted compliments on your temperament, ability, appearance and advantages

3. Deep interest signal: Express willingness to get closer, propose interactions and take the initiative to send invitations

Core Purpose of Screening

In adult social interactions, everyone wears different masks shaped by life experience, family background and social status. Many people deliver warm compliments and positive feedback out of politeness, sales goals or social etiquette rather than genuine personal interest.

Screening acts like a sieve, with two core values:
1. Distinguish genuine attraction from superficial polite flattery, confirm whether this person is worth deeper emotional investment;
2. Prevent you from misinterpreting routine friendliness as romantic interest, avoiding awkward emotional overreach and self-embarrassment.

Typical Misjudgment Case: Gym Female Trainer

Scene: The trainer actively compliments your maturity and charm, requests your WeChat to consult work issues.
Untrained mistake: Take her praise as romantic interest and launch emotional escalation rashly.
Truth: Her words are only sales tactics to induce you to purchase fitness courses.

Standard Screening Operation Logic & Three Implementation Methods

The core screening method: State your clear personal attitude or value standpoint, then observe the other party’s feedback.
• Cooperative, accommodating, responsive feedback → Pass screening, genuine interest;
• Evasive, contradictory, indifferent, ignored feedback → Fail screening, only routine social politeness.

Method 1: Direct one-sentence attitude probing (for daily online chat)

Example: Test a girl who expresses willingness to learn fitness and read with you

Screening sentence: I really love delicious food, and I hope my girlfriend can cook well. Girls who are good at cooking are super attractive to me.

Pass reaction: I’m learning cooking / I cook well myself (actively match your value).
Fail reaction: Ignore this topic, shift to other unrelated content, hold opposing views.

Method 2: Direct interest exchange screening (for offline casual scenarios)

Example: Test the sales-minded gym trainer

Screening statement: We get along pretty well, so skip selling me courses. I can give you career advice, and you teach me fitness training instead.

Pass reaction: Agree to this equal exchange without pushing sales.
Fail reaction: Find various excuses to avoid this condition and keep promoting courses.

Method 3: Story-based value screening (for offline ambiguous dinner/drinking atmosphere)

Example: Judge a lively outgoing girl’s true attitude after drinking together

Screening story: I used to only play games after work and stayed single for years. Later I realized I need to become a better person first, so I started working out, learning cooking and reading. I hope to meet someone who can exercise, cook, read and travel the world with me side by side.

Judgment standard: Any positive resonance, agreement or responsive interaction proves she passes screening. Cold, divergent replies mean she only treats you as an ordinary drinking chat partner.

Key premise of all screening: Must express your real values and true outlook on relationships; fabricated insincere statements cannot arouse genuine emotional feedback.

Three Fatal Common Misconceptions of Screening

Misconception 1: Wrong timing — Conduct screening before icebreaking & mutual understanding, before receiving any interest signals

Misconception 2: Mismatched screening intensity — Weak signals with overly strong screening, strong signals with overly weak trivial probing

Misconception 3: Invalid screening criteria — Your attitude standard cannot separate genuine interest from routine polite flattery

Misconception 1: Wrong Timing

Screening is exclusively Step 3 of the five-step chat process. It can only be launched after icebreaking + full mutual understanding, and after the other party continuously releases multi-layer interest signals. Early screening will make you look weird and presumptuous, creating a bad first impression.

Misconception 2: Mismatched Screening Strength

The intensity of your screening attitude must match the depth of her interest signals:
• Shallow signal (only praise your humor): Light screening like "Chat with me more often, I’ll keep telling you jokes"
• Deep strong signal (comprehensive praise, take initiative to meet you): Use story or directional value screening to test true intentions
Mismatched strength will lead to inaccurate judgment and missed escalation opportunities.

Misconception 3: Invalid Screening Standards

Invalid Screening Counterexample: Test the fitness trainer with "I like girls who love fitness"

Why this screening fails: The trainer naturally fits this standard as her profession, so she can easily cater to your words regardless of whether she is genuinely attracted to you. This standard cannot distinguish sales performance from real personal affection, making the screening completely meaningless.

When designing screening statements, you must set a standard that requires the other party to take extra initiative to cater to; only then can you accurately distinguish true interest from habitual social flattery.

Full Lesson Core Summary

Screening is Step 3 of the complete Five-Step Chatting Process, the core checkpoint before emotional escalation:

1. Three tiers of interest signals: information inquiry → compliments → active closeness invitations, which mark the entry point of screening;
2. Core screening logic: Output your real attitude/values, judge authenticity via the other party’s cooperative feedback;
3. Three practical screening forms: direct one-sentence probing, equal interest exchange, emotional story value output;
4. Three taboo mistakes to avoid: premature screening, mismatched screening intensity, ineffective indistinguishable screening standards.

After passing screening confirmation of genuine mutual interest, you can safely enter the fourth appreciation stage to build deeper mutual attraction; if the screening fails, pause relationship escalation and continue accumulating familiarity without rushing to confess or date invitations.
