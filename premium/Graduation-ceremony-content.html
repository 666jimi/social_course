Standard Mandatory Training Learning Method

Core rule: Follow the lesson sequence strictly, never skip any stage. Fully master and practice each lesson before advancing to the next unit.

The whole training system is tightly progressive and interlocked; skipping foundation or basic skill lessons will lead to unstable upper-stage application effects. Diligent daily practice and repeated reflection are required to form genuine social insight.

Core Question: Why The Course Does Not Provide Fixed Chat Scripts

Three fundamental reasons against memorizing fixed scripts:

1. Timeliness limitation: Scripts rely on current popular trends; they quickly become outdated and make you look disconnected from daily conversation trends later.

2. Emotional disconnection risk: Rigid script memorization separates conversation techniques from real emotional perception. You will only mechanically perform routines without understanding the other party’s inner mood, becoming a stiff "social clown."

3. Shortcut mentality damage (most critical): Chasing ready-made scripts is trying to skip practice processes and directly grab results. All genuine social ability grows from gradual thinking, observation, trial and error; copied scripts cannot adapt to your personal value and unique interaction context, and will never belong to you.

Core learning philosophy: Slow is fast. Thoroughly practice each stage’s thinking tools (three-tier progressive thinking, divergent conversation thinking, four-step response reflection) hundreds of times, and you will naturally output resonant, appropriate lines without memorizing scripts. Failures during practice are allowed; focus on analyzing underlying logic rather than seeking direct ready-made answers.
